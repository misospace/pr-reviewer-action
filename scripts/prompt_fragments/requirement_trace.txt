For every acceptance/normative requirement in the Requirement Ledger, add `enforcement`, `test`, `disposition` and `reason` to its `requirement_coverage` entry (`symbol` may name an enforcing identifier when wording differs). The validator, not you, derives the proof shape from requirement text. Locations are `{file, line}` pairs (empty arrays when none); `test` must cite a real test/fixture, never production code. Dispositions are `met|unmet|not_applicable|unverifiable`. `runtime_behavior` and `test_required` need valid enforcement and test locations plus a predicate at enforcement (comparison, guard, throw/assert, or match; not a copy); `test_required` means the text explicitly demands tests. `distributed` needs a predicate and matching enforcement and test citations in every seam. `structural_state` needs an unambiguous, non-behavioral containment/existence assertion on a named non-source file, a quoted/backticked content literal, a valid citation, and the assertion holding in the checkout; no test unless explicitly demanded. Ambiguity uses strict runtime proof. Every non-`met` needs a `reason`; each `unmet` also needs a matching finding. Use `not_applicable` only when genuinely inapplicable, and `unverifiable` when required proof cannot be established.
