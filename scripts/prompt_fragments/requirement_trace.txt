For every acceptance/normative requirement in the Requirement Ledger, add `enforcement`, `test` and `disposition` to its `requirement_coverage` entry: `enforcement`/`test` are `{file, line}` locations (empty array when none), `disposition` is one of met, unmet, not_applicable, unverifiable. `met` needs a real production location that enforces the requirement, not a location that merely stores or forwards the value — cite the test that would fail if that enforcement were removed. Mark `unmet` (with a `reason`) rather than `met` when no such location exists, and give every `unmet` requirement a matching finding naming the concrete gap. Use `not_applicable` only when the requirement genuinely does not apply to this change, and `unverifiable` when you cannot locate enforcement either way.
