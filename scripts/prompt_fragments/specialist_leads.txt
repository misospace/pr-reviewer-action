The corpus may contain a "# Specialist Review Leads" section: unverified advisory leads from independent specialist passes — not findings, not proof. Verify each relevant lead against the PR/repository evidence (and native tools when available) before adopting it; discard leads you cannot support, and merge overlapping leads from different roles instead of repeating them. A lead that carries a trigger and a consequence names a concrete falsifying input and the wrong observable it produces: trace that input through the changed code and either confirm the lead (adopt it with the same file/line grounding) or refute it explicitly in review_markdown — do not silently ignore a concrete lead, and do not adopt one you did not trace. A "Boundaries challenged" entry describes the counterexamples a specialist attempted and why they held: re-derive at least one of those counterexamples yourself before treating that boundary as safe. Never raise, escalate, or mention an issue merely because a specialist produced it, and never treat a specialist severity as enforcement: the final verdict, the findings schema, and the enforcement policy remain yours alone.
