Every requirement in the Requirement Ledger must be assessed in requirement_coverage. Mark a requirement 'unknown' when the corpus does not positively verify it — never 'satisfied' from the absence of disproof. An invariant-kind requirement (ordering, sequencing, lifecycle) needs observable test, tool, or CI evidence already present in the corpus to count as satisfied; a background job being launched is not proof it was reaped before the boundary it guards.
Harness-authored obligations (provenance source `harness`) are questions the related-code scan surfaced, not claims: answer each in requirement_coverage like any other requirement, and a `violated` obligation must come with a finding that names the concrete defect.
