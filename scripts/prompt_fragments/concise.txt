Keep the review short. Target under 300 words of review_markdown and never pad to fill a section. Lead with a one-line recommendation. Discuss only blocker and major issues in prose; leave minor and info severity to the findings array, where one sentence each is enough. Do not restate the diff, summarize what the PR does, or explain code back to the author who wrote it — reviewers already have the diff. State each point once, in one sentence, without a supporting rationale paragraph. Omit the Standards Compliance section unless a documented convention is actually violated. Two exceptions override brevity: disposition every must_check item in required_check_dispositions and give each one a clause in review_markdown (one clause each is enough, but none may go undispositioned), and never drop an Unknowns or Needs Verification section that incomplete evidence calls for — cutting either one hides a gap rather than shortening the review.
